## Rules specific to this project

- **GLP-Spec is the authority on every language issue, wherever it arises**: a language decision taken inside TGLP or IGLP is subordinate to it, and every guard, system predicate and body kernel any project adds appears in this paper's catalogue.
- **Answer a language question by pointing at the paper, never by explaining it (Udi, 2026-08-03).**  A question from another project is answered with a passage: section, and the sentence that settles it.  If answering needs a paragraph of reasoning, the paper is defective at that point — revise it until the passage alone suffices, then send the passage.
- **Which papers are the rule (Udi, 2026-08-02).**  Two papers specify the language and both are GLP-Spec's: this one and TGLP.  IGLP is the implementation paper and has derived authority only: a construct's existence, name, signature, modes and meaning are settled here or in TGLP, never in IGLP; a citation from here to IGLP points at a runtime mechanism, never at the specification of a construct.  GLP-Networking-API specifies the layer beneath the language, and citing it for what that layer does is correct.
- **Understand a requested extension before it enters the language (Udi, 2026-08-03).**  Know its reason, motivation and use from the requesting paper itself; a comment, a message or a plausible reconstruction is not knowledge.  If the reason cannot be stated in a sentence after reading, the extension is not ready and the question goes back to the requester.
- **The paper is the sole rule (Udi, 2026-08-02).**  Where code disagrees with the paper, the code is wrong: report it as non-compliant to its owner, through Integration.  No sentence of the specification may rest on an implementation limitation; any mechanism in the implementation that gates what the specification permits is a defect by construction; a test asserting behaviour the paper forbids is a wrong test.  **Absence in the code is not evidence about the language**: measure the code to learn what conforms, never to learn what the language is.
- **Every language ruling is Udi's to approve before it is issued (Udi, 2026-08-02).**  A ruling is anything that decides what GLP is: whether a construct exists or is removed, its name, signature or modes, what a guard, kernel or system predicate means, and which paper carries a subject.  Put it to him with the measurement, the alternatives and what each costs, as one recommendation, and wait; never post it to another project's inbox, write it into a paper or instruct a Code session on it before he has answered.  Answering what the language already says is not a ruling.
- **Propose; do not "rule" (Udi, 2026-08-03).**  One recommendation for his approval, with the alternatives and their costs, numbered so he can answer briefly.
- The seam predicates `self_key/1`, `sign/3`, `signature/2` and `decompose_module/4` are in the catalogue; `verify/3`, `sign/2`, `signed/4`, `signed/2` and `valid_attestation/4` are gone.  `signature/2` reports `signed(Key, Hash, _)` or `unsigned` rather than failing on a forgery (Udi, 2026-09-18).
