## What the papers gained today

TGLP, `99332fc`:

- Definition (Instantiation): a map from a callee's parameters to types of the program under which the caller's clause and the callee's clauses are well-typed, and every input path of the instantiated declaration is accepted by some clause.  It replaces the call-site matching rule, which failed on a constructed argument.  Coverage is what selects θ; there is no least-θ rule.
- An anonymous variable at a produced position of a clause head is written `_?` and is an output the clause never produces; an anonymous reader at a consumed position stays forbidden.
- Definition (Constant Type) and Proposition (Readers of Constant Types): a reader of a constant type may occur more than once.  It replaces a ground-type sentence of mine that admitted `Stream(Integer)`, and it is Remark "Guards and SRSW" with the groundness coming from the type.
- A guard narrows the head occurrence it tests, by the meet of the two types, and the body is checked under the meet.
- A parametric imported declaration instantiates at the call as a local one does.
- `MutualRef` is a primitive type, with its row in the consistency table; it is not a `Constant`.
