## Open with Udi: the cGLP semantics does not admit the Remark "Guards and SRSW" (Blocking: yes)

The Remark lets X and X? occur many times in a clause whose guard implies X? ground (`831f315`, Udi's sentence restored 2026-10-12).  The definitions of Section "Operational Semantics" and the appendices were written for clauses that satisfy SO, and do not follow it:
1. A repeated reader in a body: the new goal of Reduce holds X? twice, so it fails SO and is not a goal (Definition "GLP Program, Goals"), and by Definition "cGLP Transition System" no Reduce takes such a clause.  The catalogue relies on such clauses: `send_to_net`'s passes Q? three times (`appendix-guards.tex`, the paragraph on `send_to_net`).
2. A repeated reader in a head: `copy(X, X?, X?)` against `copy(3, Y, Z)` needs {Y := X?, Z := X?}, which breaks the joint SO a writers substitution requires (Definition "Substitutions and Assignments"), so there is no writer mgu.
3. Definition "Term Matching" (`appendix-term-matching.tex`) is defined only for terms that jointly satisfy SO.  The proof of SO Preservation (`appendix-proofs.tex`) says "Since C satisfies SRSW, it satisfies SO", "by SO of C", and "a clause variable occurs once in its clause", each false for such a clause; the proof of Monotonicity assumes one head writer at the position of each goal reader.
4. Not yet put to Udi: Section "Guards" says the reduction is "augmented to succeed if the guard also succeeds", and does not say under which substitution the guard is evaluated.

GLP #5's recommendation, put to Udi 2026-10-12 08:20 UTC and unanswered: at a reduction, a variable whose reader a groundness-implying guard tests is replaced by its ground value, writer and reader alike, in head and body; one clause in Definition "Writer MGU" and one in the Reduce rule.  The new goal then satisfies SO, and with it come Term Matching's row for a later occurrence of a head variable (matched against the value of the earlier one) and the cases of the two proofs.  Nothing goes into the paper before he answers.
